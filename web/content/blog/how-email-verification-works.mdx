description: A short guide to the signals shown by Verifier.
author: Verifier

category: product
---

An email check looks at more than the address format. Verifier reports syntax, domain and mail exchanger records, and mailbox signals where they are available.

The result also includes a quality score, a safe-to-send indicator, and reason codes. Review the reasons before deciding how to use an address. A network or mail server may refuse to confirm whether a mailbox exists, so a result can remain uncertain.

You can check one address in the [workspace](/app/check) or upload a CSV in [lists](/app/lists). Each workspace has its own results and list history.
